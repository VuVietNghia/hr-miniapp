# Terms of operation

Version: 2.0.0 — 2026-08-30

The deploying organization is responsible for lawful collection and use of recruitment, employee, email, and payroll data; valid notices and consents; Room membership and permission administration; EmailJS configuration; retention; incident response; and compliance with employment, privacy, tax, and banking obligations.

Only an authorized operator may pair, publish, approve, install, or rotate this app. Verify the Hub fingerprint out of band, protect standalone identity files with owner-only permissions, and never reuse development OAuth credentials in managed or standalone production.

Optional PrivOS permissions may be revoked. The documented result is feature degradation, not a promise of availability. Payroll remains unavailable unless the trusted backend Owner policy is proven; client roles, payloads, and UI state never substitute for backend authorization.

Version 2.0.0 preserves the existing HR business contracts but changes platform/runtime boundaries. Real deployment acceptance, downstream persistence verification, and rollback rehearsal are operator responsibilities before production use.
